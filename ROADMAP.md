# Roadmap

Book Skills Creator 2.0 includes shared explicit discovery, configurable limits, structured TXT/Markdown/DOCX/HTML/EPUB extraction, optional local PDF text extraction, evidence IDs and hashes, a canonical pack manifest, deterministic generation and graph validation, behavioral case artifacts, and incremental impact reporting. It does not perform automatic semantic regeneration.

Next priorities are stronger format fidelity where evidence warrants it, a bounded and more configurable PDF adapter, better duplicate-capability review aids, and richer agent-evaluated behavioral cases. OCR, page-layout reconstruction, and provider-specific adapters remain optional future work. These roadmap items do not imply a release date or version.
